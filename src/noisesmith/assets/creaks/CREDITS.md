the results are free to use anywhere, including commercial projects.

## What was done to the files

Mono, resampled to 48 kHz, silence trimmed from both ends, leveled to a peak of -1 dBFS, 5 ms
edge fades, saved as 16-bit WAV. See `scripts/prepare_creak_assets.py`. Nothing else was changed.

## A note on provenance

Each source page declares CC0 1.0. Those statements, and the claim that a recording is the
uploader's own, were made by the uploaders and cannot be independently verified. If you are the
rights holder of any of these recordings and want one removed, please open an issue.
